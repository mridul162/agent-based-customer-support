# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Options

| Option | Delivery Time | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Delivery | 2–5 business days | Nationwide |
| Express Delivery | 1–2 business days | Select major cities |
| Same-Day Delivery | Same day (order before 12 PM) | Dhaka only |

## Shipping Charges

- Free standard shipping on orders above a set minimum threshold (shown at checkout).
- Express and same-day delivery incur an additional fee, calculated at checkout based on location.

## Order Processing

- Orders are processed and packed within 24 hours of confirmation (excluding public holidays).
- You will receive a confirmation SMS/email once your order ships, including a tracking number.

## Delivery Attempts

- Our courier partner will attempt delivery up to 2 times.
- If delivery fails both times, the order is returned to Silico and the customer is contacted to arrange redelivery or cancellation.

## Delays

- Delivery times may be affected by weather, courier disruptions, or remote locations. Customers will be notified of any significant delay.

## Order Inspection on Delivery

- Customers are encouraged to inspect the package at the time of delivery. Report any visible damage to the delivery agent and to Silico support within 24 hours.
