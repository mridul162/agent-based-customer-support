# Returns Policy

## Eligibility

- Items may be returned within **7 days of delivery**.
- Phones must be unused, with original packaging, box contents, and all accessories included.
- IMEI/serial number must match the original invoice.
- Accessories must be unused and in original, unopened packaging.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Screen protectors and other consumables once opened
- SIM cards or activated eSIM products
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page
- Products with damaged, missing, or altered original packaging

## How to Request a Return

1. Go to "My Orders" and select "Request Return," or contact support with your order number.
2. Choose a reason for return and, if applicable, upload photos of the issue.
3. Once approved, a courier will be scheduled to pick up the item, or you may drop it off at a Silico service point.

## Inspection

- Returned items are inspected upon receipt to confirm condition and completeness.
- Items that fail inspection (e.g., signs of use, missing parts) will be sent back to the customer and are not eligible for refund/exchange.

## Exchanges

- Exchanges for a different color/variant of the same product are allowed within the 7-day return window, subject to stock availability.
