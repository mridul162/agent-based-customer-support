# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of delivery.
Refunds are processed after product inspection.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Refund Timeline

| Payment Method | Refund Time After Approval |
|---|---|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5–7 business days |
| Mobile Wallet (bKash/Nagad/Rocket) | 2–3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 3–5 business days |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Refunded via bank transfer or mobile wallet, 5–7 business days |

## Partial Refunds

- Partial refunds may be issued if only part of an order is returned, or if a promotional discount applied to a bundle is affected by a partial return.

## Shipping Fees

- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a Silico error (wrong item shipped, defective product, etc.).
- Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items; otherwise, return shipping cost may be deducted from the refund.

## Failed or Delayed Refunds

- If a refund has not been received within the stated timeline, customers should contact support with their order number and refund reference (if available) for investigation.
