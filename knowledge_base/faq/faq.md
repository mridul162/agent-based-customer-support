# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Q: How do I place an order?**
Browse products on the Silico website or app, add items to your cart, and complete checkout using any of our supported payment methods.

**Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement, as long as the order has not yet been packed for shipping. Contact support immediately with your order number.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking link by email and SMS. You can also check order status under "My Orders" in your account.

## Products

**Q: Are your phones brand new and original?**
Yes. All phones sold by Silico are 100% brand new, sealed, and sourced directly from authorized distributors or manufacturers.

**Q: Do you sell refurbished phones?**
Select refurbished/certified pre-owned phones are available and are clearly labeled as "Refurbished" on the product page, with grading details (A/B/C condition).

**Q: Do accessories come with a warranty?**
Most accessories carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Phones carry the manufacturer's standard warranty (see Warranty Policy).

## Shipping

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on your location. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is available in select cities.

**Q: Do you ship nationwide?**
Yes, we ship to all districts. Remote areas may experience 1–2 additional business days.

## Returns & Refunds

**Q: Can I return a phone if I change my mind?**
Yes, unused and unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See our Returns Policy for full details.

**Q: How long do refunds take?**
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item passes inspection.

## Payments

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept credit/debit cards, mobile wallets (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), bank transfers, and cash on delivery (COD) in eligible areas.

**Q: Is it safe to pay online on your site?**
Yes. All online payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways with SSL encryption.

## Account & Support

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email or phone number.

**Q: How can I contact customer support?**
You can reach us via live chat, email at support@silicomobile.com, or phone at our support hotline, available 9 AM–9 PM, 7 days a week.
