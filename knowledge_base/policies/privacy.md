# Privacy Policy

## Information We Collect

- Contact details: name, phone number, email, delivery address
- Order history and payment confirmation status (Silico does not store full card numbers)
- Device/browser information for site security and performance

## How We Use Your Information

- To process and deliver orders
- To provide customer support
- To send order updates, promotional offers (opt-out available), and service notices

## Data Sharing

- Shared with delivery partners solely for the purpose of fulfilling shipments
- Shared with payment gateways solely for processing transactions
- Never sold to third parties for marketing purposes

## Data Security

- All online transactions use SSL encryption
- Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant gateways; Silico does not store full card details

## Customer Rights

- Customers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting support@silicomobile.com
- Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days, subject to legal record-keeping requirements
