# General Store Policies

## About Silico

Silico Mobile Phone and Accessories Shop sells new and certified refurbished smartphones, along with a wide range of mobile accessories including cases, chargers, earphones, power banks, and screen protectors.

## Order Acceptance

- All orders are subject to acceptance and availability.
- Silico reserves the right to cancel any order due to pricing errors, suspected fraud, or stock unavailability. Customers will be notified and fully refunded in such cases.

## Pricing

- Prices are listed in local currency and are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.
- Prices may change without prior notice, but confirmed orders will honor the price at the time of purchase.

## Product Authenticity

- All new phones are sourced from authorized distributors and manufacturers.
- Refurbished products are inspected, graded, and clearly labeled as such.
- Silico does not sell counterfeit or "master copy" devices.

## Customer Conduct

- Abusive or threatening behavior toward support staff will not be tolerated and may result in account suspension.
- Fraudulent return or refund claims may result in permanent account suspension and legal action.

## Privacy

- Customer data is collected only for order processing, delivery, and support purposes.
- Silico does not sell customer data to third parties. See our full Privacy Policy for details.

## Changes to Policies

Silico reserves the right to update these policies at any time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
