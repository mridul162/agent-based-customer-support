# Product Catalog Overview

## Categories

### Smartphones
- Latest flagship and mid-range models from major brands (Samsung, Apple, Xiaomi, Realme, Vivo, Oppo, and others)
- Both brand-new and certified refurbished options available
- Filter by brand, price range, RAM/storage, and 5G support on the website

### Accessories
- **Cases & Covers** — silicone, leather, rugged, and transparent options for most popular models
- **Chargers & Cables** — fast chargers, wireless chargers, USB-C/Lightning cables
- **Earphones & Headphones** — wired, true wireless (TWS), and Bluetooth neckbands
- **Power Banks** — 10,000mAh to 30,000mAh, with fast-charging support
- **Screen Protectors** — tempered glass and hydrogel film, model-specific fitting

## Product Information

- Each product page lists full specifications, in-box contents, warranty duration, and available color/storage variants.
- Stock availability is shown in real time; out-of-stock items can be "notified" via email when restocked.
- Genuine product images are used; actual color may vary slightly due to display settings.

## Compatibility

- Accessory pages list compatible phone models. Please verify your exact model number before ordering cases or screen protectors.
- Chargers indicate wattage and fast-charging protocol compatibility (e.g., PD, QC).

## Bundles & Offers

- Phone + accessory bundles are available at a discount on select listings.
- Ongoing promotions are listed on the Offers page and applied automatically at checkout, or via promo code where specified.
