# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods

- **Credit/Debit Cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and other major cards
- **Mobile Wallets** — bKash, Nagad, Rocket
- **Bank Transfer** — direct transfer to Silico's business account
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** — available in eligible areas, up to a maximum order value

## Payment Security

- All online payments are processed via PCI-DSS compliant, SSL-encrypted payment gateways.
- Silico does not store full card numbers or CVV codes.

## Order Confirmation

- Orders paid online are confirmed automatically once payment is verified.
- Bank transfer orders are confirmed manually within 1 business day of payment verification; please share the transaction reference with support to speed this up.
- COD orders are confirmed upon successful phone verification.

## Failed or Pending Payments

- If a payment fails but funds are deducted, the amount is automatically reversed by the payment gateway within 5–10 business days.
- If an order shows "Payment Pending" for more than 24 hours, contact support with your transaction ID.

## Installment Plans

- EMI/installment plans are available on select phones through partner banks and cards. Eligibility and terms are shown at checkout for qualifying products.

## Invoices

- A digital invoice/receipt is emailed automatically after each successful order and is also available under "My Orders."
